Maintenance windows

{{if $v.IsAdmin}}
New window New freeze
{{end}}

A window is when changes may run: it opens on a cron schedule in its time zone and stays open for its duration, for every host or only the hosts in its groups. A change or patch group tied to a window starts jobs only while it's open; a job that wouldn't finish before it closes waits for the next opening. A freeze has the same shape and blocks every change except Emergency ones on the hosts it covers. Times below are in {{$v.DisplayTZ}}; the calendar shows them by day.{{if $v.IsAdmin}} Click a window's name to edit it.{{end}}
