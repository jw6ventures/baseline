Everything that changes a host runs as a job inside a change record. A Normal change runs once approvers approve it (self-approval is allowed and recorded as such), inside its maintenance window if it has one. Standard changes are pre-approved by their change model; Emergency ones need one approver and run even during a freeze. Emergency changes, and changes that failed, close after a review.
