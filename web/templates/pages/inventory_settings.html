Missing Compose stacks

A stack whose containers are gone from its host is marked missing after an hour. Missing stacks stay on the Gone list until you delete them, unless Baseline retires them for you.

{{if .Body.RetireDays}}On: stacks missing for more than {{plural .Body.RetireDays "day"}} are retired.{{else}}Off: missing stacks are never retired automatically.{{end}} Retiring hides a stack from the stack lists and its host's Stacks tab. Nothing is deleted: its settings, history and relationships are kept, the Retired view still lists it, and it turns active again by itself if its host reports it. Checked every 10 minutes; each retirement is in the audit log.
