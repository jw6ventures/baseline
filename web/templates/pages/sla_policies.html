SLA policies

A policy sets response and resolution targets by priority. A new work item takes the most specific enabled policy: its project and ticket type, then its project, then its ticket type, then the policy for everything. The response clock stops when the item first leaves To do; the resolution clock stops when it is done, and runs again if it's reopened. Changing a policy affects new items only; a priority change on an item moves its running targets.
